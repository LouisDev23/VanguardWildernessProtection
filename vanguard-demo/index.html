Who we are

A specialist in wilderness security.

Vanguard Wilderness Protection is a specialist wildlife protection and wilderness security service provider focused on safeguarding wildlife and protecting the properties and environments in which they live.

We understand that wilderness security requires more than conventional security measures. It requires knowledge of the environment, awareness of animal behaviour, tracking abilities and the ability to identify and respond to potential threats.

Our approach combines security experience, wildlife knowledge and practical field skills to provide protection solutions suited to the needs of each property.

Our mission

To protect.

To protect wildlife and natural environments through professional security services, proactive monitoring and responsible field operations.

Our vision

To be trusted.

To become a trusted name in wildlife protection and wilderness security, contributing to the long-term preservation of wildlife and the security of conservation areas.

Our values

Five principles carried into every patrol.

Our approach

Every property has its own challenges.

We aim to understand the specific requirements of each client and develop suitable security solutions. Our focus is on prevention, observation, reporting and a professional response to potential security threats.
